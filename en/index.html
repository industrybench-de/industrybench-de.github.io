How well do AI models know German industrial and safety rules?

This is what IndustryBench-DE measures: an exam for AI models with questions from everyday industrial work in Germany, in mechanical, electrical, chemical and energy engineering.

Why? More and more engineers and technicians ask AI assistants for advice. A wrong answer can be costly or dangerous there, for example when an AI approves a device for a room with explosive gas where it must not be used. So the benchmark shows three things: how much the models know about German and European industrial rules, whether they apply these rules correctly to a concrete case, and how often they recommend something unsafe.

Question types

Four kinds of question

Every question describes a situation from practice and belongs to one of four types. Each type appears in all four areas.

  • Standards & terms

    What does a marking, a class or a limit value mean, and what follows from it in a concrete case?

    For exampleWhat does the rating plate mean for the planned use?

  • Selection & substitution

    Which product, variant or material suits the operating conditions? May one part be replaced by another?

    For exampleWhich of two motors meets the EU energy-efficiency rules?

  • Safety & compliance

    Is a rule being broken? Which duty, inspection or protective measure applies?

    For exampleIs this pump allowed in a room where gas could explode?

  • Fault diagnosis

    What is the most likely cause of a fault, and how is it fixed safely?

    For exampleThe alarm goes off but the display looks normal. What is wrong?

The examples only show the kind of question. The real questions stay private so they do not end up in AI training data. How a question is built
